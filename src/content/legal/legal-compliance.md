## 1\. Terms and Conditions of Business

### About these terms

These terms apply to purchases made through Hair Medics UK Shop. They should be read together with the Privacy Policy, Returns and Refunds Policy, Delivery Policy, Medical and Prescribing Terms, Website Disclaimer and Cookie Policy. Nothing in these terms excludes or restricts a consumer right that cannot lawfully be excluded.

The legal name of the business operating the shop, registered office, company number, trading address, email address and other legally required contact details should be displayed on the website and inserted into this policy before publication.

Customers must provide accurate, complete and current information. Where a product is age-restricted, prescription-only, pharmacy-only, clinically assessed or otherwise regulated, the customer must satisfy all applicable eligibility, identity, clinical and legal requirements.

### Orders and contract formation

An order is an offer to buy. An automated acknowledgement does not necessarily mean the order has been accepted. Acceptance normally occurs when the business confirms acceptance or dispatch. Orders may be declined or cancelled where products are unavailable, payment fails, fraud is suspected, information is materially inaccurate, or legal, regulatory or patient-safety requirements are not met.

Prices are stated in pounds sterling unless otherwise shown. Applicable taxes and delivery charges will be disclosed as required before checkout. Customers must use an authorised payment method. A legitimate statutory or contractual right to dispute a payment is not restricted by these terms.

### Products

Reasonable care is taken with descriptions and images, but packaging and presentation may change. Products must be used in accordance with instructions, warnings and, where applicable, prescribing directions. Individual results from cosmetic, hair-care or hair-loss products vary.

### Medicines and regulated products

Nothing on the website or in a customer disclaimer permits the unlawful advertising, prescribing, importation, sale or supply of a medicine. Any medicine that requires a prescription, pharmacist involvement, clinical assessment or other authorisation must only be supplied when the applicable requirements have been met.

### Liability

Nothing excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded, including liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, or statutory obligations relating to faulty or misdescribed goods. Subject to law, the business is not responsible for losses that were not reasonably foreseeable when the contract was made.

### Governing law

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales. Consumers retain any mandatory rights and jurisdiction protections that apply to them in the country in which they live.

## 2\. Returns, Refunds and Cancellation Policy

### Cancellation rights

Where statutory distance-selling cancellation rights apply, customers may cancel within the applicable statutory period. The business will provide the information required by law and will not contract out of mandatory consumer rights.

### Health and hygiene exception

Certain sealed goods may cease to be eligible for cancellation once unsealed where they are not suitable for return for health-protection or hygiene reasons. This exception will only be relied on where it lawfully applies.

### Medicines

Medicines may be subject to additional safety restrictions on return or reuse. Customers should contact the shop before returning any medicine. This does not remove rights relating to faulty, damaged, incorrect or misdescribed goods.

### Faulty, damaged or incorrect items

Customers should contact the shop promptly and provide reasonable evidence such as photographs, packaging or batch information where requested. Where the law requires a remedy, the business will provide the appropriate repair, replacement, price reduction or refund.

### Refund method

Approved refunds will normally be made to the original payment method unless another method is agreed or required by law.

## 4\. Medical and Prescribing Terms

### General

Website information is general information and is not, by itself, an individual diagnosis or prescription. A purchase request does not guarantee that a medicine or treatment will be clinically appropriate.

### Clinical assessment

Where a clinical assessment is required, the customer must provide truthful and complete information about relevant medical history, medicines, allergies and other requested information. A suitably authorised clinician must make any prescribing decision independently.

### Prescription-only medicines

Prescription-only medicines must only be supplied through a lawful route and on the basis of a valid prescription where required. Commercial staff must not override a clinician’s decision.

### Unlicensed or off-label use

Where an authorised clinician considers an unlicensed medicine or an off-label use, the decision and supply must comply with applicable medicines law, professional standards and informed-consent requirements. A customer waiver cannot legalise an otherwise unlawful supply.

### Safety

Customers must follow directions supplied with a medicine or given by their clinician, must not share prescribed medicines, and should seek appropriate medical assistance for serious or unexpected reactions.

### No guaranteed outcome

Hair-loss treatment response varies. No particular level of regrowth, prevention of future loss, or treatment timescale is guaranteed unless an express lawful written guarantee is given.

## 6\. Privacy Policy (UK)

### Data controller

The legal entity operating Hair Medics UK Shop should identify itself as controller and publish its registered/business address and privacy contact details before this policy goes live.

### Information collected

Depending on the service, information may include identity and contact details, account and order data, payment-related information, correspondence, delivery details, device/website information and, where clinically necessary, health information.

### Health information

Health data is special-category personal data and requires additional protection. Where health information is processed, the business must identify an Article 6 lawful basis and an applicable Article 9 condition, together with any additional Data Protection Act 2018 requirements.

### Purposes

Information may be used to process orders, provide customer service, arrange lawful clinical or prescribing services, meet legal and regulatory obligations, prevent fraud, maintain records, improve services and send marketing where permitted.

### Sharing

Information may be shared where necessary with appropriate service providers such as payment processors, delivery providers, IT providers and, where relevant, authorised clinicians, pharmacies or other regulated providers. Sharing must be limited to what is necessary and supported by an appropriate legal basis and contractual safeguards.

### Retention and security

Personal information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the relevant purpose and legal obligations. Appropriate technical and organisational security measures should be used, with particular care for health data.

### Individual rights

Individuals may have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability and rights relating to certain automated decisions, subject to statutory conditions and exemptions. The privacy notice should explain how to exercise these rights and how to complain to the ICO.

### International transfers

Where personal data is transferred outside the UK, the business must use a lawful transfer mechanism and appropriate safeguards where required.

## 7\. Cookie Policy

### Cookies

The website may use strictly necessary cookies and, where appropriate, analytics, functionality or advertising cookies. Non-essential cookies should not be placed before valid consent where consent is required.

### Cookie controls

Visitors should be given a clear mechanism to accept, reject or manage non-essential cookies and should be able to change their preferences later.

### Third parties

Where third-party services place cookies or similar technologies, the policy and consent interface should identify the relevant purposes and providers as required by law.

## 8\. Complaints Policy

### How to complain

Customers should be given a clear email and postal contact for complaints. The business should acknowledge and investigate complaints fairly and within a reasonable timeframe.

### Clinical complaints

Complaints concerning prescribing or clinical care should be referred to the clinician or regulated provider responsible for that element of care, while the shop remains responsible for handling any part of the complaint that concerns its own service.

### Safety concerns

Product safety concerns, suspected adverse reactions, recalls or regulatory issues should be escalated promptly under the business’s applicable safety and regulatory procedures.

## 9\. Publication and Compliance Checklist

### Complete business identity

Insert the exact legal entity name, company number, registered office, trading address, customer-service email and telephone number.

### Map the supply chain

Record who owns the website, who takes payment, who conducts any clinical assessment, who prescribes, who dispenses/supplies, where stock is located and who ships each regulated product.

### Check medicine status

Classify every medicinal product by its UK legal status and verify that the advertising, prescribing, dispensing, import/export and supply model is lawful before listing it.

### Privacy mapping

Document Article 6 lawful bases, Article 9 conditions for health data, retention periods, processors, international transfers and whether a DPIA or other documentation is required.

### Checkout compliance

Ensure required pre-contract information, total price, delivery charges, cancellation information and contract confirmation are provided in a durable form.

### Keep policies aligned

Product pages, checkout wording, consent forms, medical questionnaires, privacy notices, returns wording and these terms should not contradict one another.
