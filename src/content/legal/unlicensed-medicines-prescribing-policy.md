**1\. Purpose of this policy**

Hair Medics UK is committed to ensuring that medicines associated with our treatment services are prescribed, supplied and communicated responsibly and in accordance with applicable UK medicines legislation and regulatory guidance.

This policy explains our approach where an appropriately qualified healthcare professional considers that an unlicensed medicine may be clinically appropriate for an individual patient.

**2\. What is an unlicensed medicine?**

An unlicensed medicine is a medicinal product that does not hold a UK marketing authorisation for the particular product concerned.

The absence of a marketing authorisation does not necessarily mean that a medicine cannot lawfully be prescribed or supplied. UK legislation provides circumstances in which an unlicensed medicinal product may be supplied to meet the special clinical needs of an individual patient.

Unlicensed medicines have not been assessed and authorised by the MHRA in the same manner as medicines holding a UK marketing authorisation.

**3\. Individual clinical assessment**

Hair Medics UK does not guarantee that any particular prescription medicine or unlicensed medicine will be provided to a patient.

Where prescription treatment is available through our service, the patient must first undergo an appropriate clinical assessment.

The prescribing decision is made by an appropriately qualified prescriber based upon factors including:

-   the patient’s individual clinical circumstances;
-   medical and medication history;
-   suitability for treatment;
-   contraindications and potential interactions;
-   available licensed treatment options; and
-   whether there is a genuine clinical need for the proposed treatment.

Completing a consultation or making a payment associated with a consultation does not guarantee that a prescription will be issued.

**4\. Use of unlicensed medicines**

Where an unlicensed medicine is considered, it will only be prescribed where the responsible prescriber determines that its use is clinically appropriate for the individual patient and that applicable requirements for prescribing and supply have been satisfied.

Where appropriate, patients will be informed that the medicine is unlicensed.

The decision to prescribe remains a clinical decision of the responsible prescriber and is not determined by Hair Medics UK’s commercial interests.

**5\. Licensed alternatives**

Where a suitable licensed medicine is available that can meet the patient’s clinical needs, this will be considered as part of the prescribing process.

An unlicensed medicine should not be supplied merely because a patient requests a particular formulation, concentration or product.

The clinical needs of the individual patient are the determining consideration.

**6\. Prescription-only medicines**

Prescription-only medicines cannot be supplied without a valid prescription from an appropriately qualified prescriber.

Patients must not regard information appearing on this website as a prescription, recommendation or confirmation that a particular medicine is suitable for them.

A prescription will only be issued following an appropriate clinical assessment and where the prescriber considers treatment clinically appropriate.

**7\. Advertising and website information**

Hair Medics UK does not intend information on this website concerning prescription treatment to constitute advertising of prescription-only or unlicensed medicines to members of the public.

Information concerning our services is intended to explain the nature of the clinical service available.

We do not intend to promote an unlicensed medicine to members of the public or encourage patients to request a particular unlicensed medicine.

Any factual information concerning medicines should not be interpreted as a claim that a particular medicine is safe, effective or appropriate for every patient.

Treatment decisions are made individually following clinical assessment.

**8\. No self-selection of prescription treatment**

Patients cannot require a prescriber to issue a particular medicine simply by selecting or requesting it through the website.

Where our website permits a patient to indicate a treatment preference, that preference forms part of the consultation only.

The prescriber may:

-   approve treatment;
-   recommend an alternative treatment;
-   request further medical information;
-   require additional assessment; or
-   decline to prescribe.

Patient safety and clinical appropriateness take priority over the patient’s requested treatment.

9\. Pharmacy and supply

Where a prescription is issued, prescription medicines must be dispensed and supplied through an appropriately authorised and lawful supply chain.

Hair Medics UK will not represent itself as a registered pharmacy unless the relevant business and premises are appropriately registered to operate as such.

Where an independent pharmacy or other authorised supplier fulfils a prescription, responsibility for dispensing and supply remains subject to the professional and regulatory obligations applicable to that provider.

10\. Imported or specially manufactured medicines

Additional regulatory requirements may apply where an unlicensed medicine is specially manufactured or imported into the United Kingdom.

Where applicable, medicines must be manufactured, imported, distributed and supplied through organisations holding the licences or authorisations required by UK legislation.

A patient disclaimer or consent form does not replace these regulatory requirements.

**11\. Patient information and consent**

Where an unlicensed medicine is prescribed, appropriate information should be provided to enable the patient to make an informed decision about treatment.

Depending upon the treatment, this may include:

-   the unlicensed status of the medicine;
-   why the treatment is being considered;
-   expected benefits;
-   material risks and potential adverse effects;
-   contraindications;
-   alternative treatment options;
-   instructions for use; and
-   circumstances in which medical advice should be sought.

Patients should read all information provided with their medication and follow the instructions given by their prescriber and dispensing provider.

**12\. Side effects and adverse reactions**

All medicines can cause side effects.

Patients experiencing unexpected or significant adverse effects should seek appropriate medical advice.

Serious or potentially life-threatening symptoms require urgent medical attention.

Suspected adverse drug reactions may also be reported through the MHRA Yellow Card Scheme where appropriate.

**13\. Treatment outcomes**

Hair Medics UK does not guarantee a particular clinical or cosmetic outcome from medication.

Responses to hair-loss treatment vary between individuals and can be affected by the underlying cause of hair loss, genetics, adherence to treatment, duration of treatment and other medical factors.

**14\. Website information is not medical advice**

General information provided through the Hair Medics UK website is for informational purposes and does not replace an individual consultation with an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

Patients should not start, discontinue or alter prescription medication solely because of information appearing on our website.

**15\. Regulatory compliance**

Hair Medics UK aims to operate its medicinal treatment services in accordance with applicable requirements including the Human Medicines Regulations 2012 and relevant guidance issued by the Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency (MHRA).

Our policies and website content may be amended where necessary to reflect changes in legislation, regulatory guidance or our clinical services.

**16\. Contact**

Questions concerning this policy or our prescribing process may be submitted to Hair Medics UK through the contact details provided on our website.
