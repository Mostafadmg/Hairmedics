Logo

The mark shows three hair strands rising from the scalp, each taller than the last — hair, and growth over time. The wordmark is set in Fraunces SemiBold with “UK” in Manrope, tracked and in copper.

Do

  • Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the mark’s strands.
  • Use the horizontal logo at a minimum of 120px wide on screen, 30mm in print.
  • Use the reversed logo on Midnight Ink or photography darker than 60%.

Don’t

  • Stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows to the logo.
  • Recolour the mark or wordmark outside the palette.
  • Set “Hair Medics” in another font — use the supplied SVG files.

Voice & tone

We talk like a knowledgeable clinician who is also a decent human being. We never overpromise, never shame, and always explain.

Clinical, not cold

“Your prescription is reviewed by a GMC-registered doctor.”

“Our docs will sort you out!”

Honest about results

“Treatment usually needs 3–6 months before you see a benefit.”

“Cure your hair loss in weeks!”

Reassuring and human

“Hair loss is common — and there’s a lot we can do about it.”

“Embarrassed by your bald spot?”

Plain English

“A medicine that lowers DHT, the hormone linked to hair loss.”

“A 5α-reductase inhibitor modulating androgenic pathways.”

Writing rules

  • Sentence case for headings and buttons — never ALL CAPS.
  • Write “minoxidil”, not “Minoxidil”, mid-sentence (it isn’t a brand name). Product names keep their capitals.
  • Avoid “cure”, “guaranteed” and “miracle”. Say what a treatment may do, and how long it takes.
  • Don’t promote prescription-only medicines in ads or social posts — talk about the consultation, not the drug.
  • British English: colour, personalised, centre, programme.

Imagery & iconography

Photography

Real, relaxed people with natural smiles in warm, soft light. Prefer light, airy backgrounds (Bone, Linen) over black studio backdrops. Show men and women, of different ages and ethnicities.

Product shots

Square, centred, on pure white with soft shadow. The product should fill ~70% of the frame so it reads at thumbnail size.

Icons

Lucide line icons, 1.75px stroke, rounded caps. Icons support labels — they never replace them.
