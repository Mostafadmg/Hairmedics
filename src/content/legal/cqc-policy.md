Hair Medics UK is a brand name  is NOT Registered with the CQC as a brand, however it’s sister company known as [Skin Medics UK](https://skinmedicsuk.com/) is registered with CQC and your entire patient journey will be managed by Skin Medics UK.

All locations listed on Hair Medics UK are owned by its sister company known as Skin Medics UK.

Hair Medics UK in its self is brand designated to providing patients with information and introduction to its sister company known as Skin Medics UK.
