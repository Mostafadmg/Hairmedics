About this product

Active Ingredients

Each tablet contains:

Dutasteride 0.5mg
Dutasteride is a 5-alpha-reductase inhibitor that reduces the conversion of testosterone to dihydrotestosterone (DHT). DHT plays an important role in the progressive miniaturisation of susceptible hair follicles associated with male pattern hair loss.

Minoxidil 2.5mg
Minoxidil may help promote hair growth by supporting hair follicles and prolonging the active growth (anagen) phase of the hair cycle.

Important Safety Information

This medicine is not suitable for everyone. A clinical assessment is required before treatment to determine whether it is appropriate for you.

Dutasteride can cause side effects including reduced libido, erectile dysfunction, ejaculation disorders and breast tenderness or enlargement. Mood-related adverse effects may also require medical assessment.

Oral minoxidil can cause side effects including dizziness, fluid retention, swelling, increased heart rate and changes in blood pressure. Other adverse effects are possible.

Women who are pregnant or may become pregnant should not handle leaking capsules or crushed/broken tablets containing dutasteride because dutasteride can be absorbed through the skin and may affect the development of a male fetus.

Always take this medicine exactly as prescribed. Do not exceed the prescribed dose.
